Privacy &
how it works

What the app does

Figure Finder reads the Google Slides presentation you select. AI decides where an image would help, searches for a suitable image, and adds it with a caption and source citation. Clicking “Add figures” authorizes edits to that presentation. No payment is required from users.

Google access

We request access to individual files you select through Google’s picker. Your Google password is never sent to Figure Finder. Temporary Google access tokens are held in memory during a run; we do not save refresh tokens. The shared invitation password is separate from your Google account.

Where content goes

Slide screenshots, relevant slide text and candidate images are sent to OpenAI for analysis. Topic-based search queries are sent to Brave and Wikimedia. Images are downloaded from their source websites. Google receives the image URLs and slide edits. These services handle their requests under their own policies. Use presentations you have permission to process with these services.

Temporary storage

Working screenshots, images and search logs are stored temporarily on the server during a run and removed when the run exits normally. Run summaries, candidate explanations and small image previews are available only to the browser session that created them, for up to 6 hours. You can clear completed runs individually or sign out to clear your history. The free host may restart and erase sessions and history earlier. Infrastructure request logs may be retained by the hosting provider; we do not intentionally log tokens or slide content to them.

Disconnecting

“Disconnect Google” revokes the app’s Google access. You can also remove access in your Google account connections. Clearing history or disconnecting does not remove figures already added to your slides. You can edit or delete those in Google Slides.

Image sources

Citations identify image sources and include available attribution. A citation does not itself grant permission to reuse an image. Check the linked source and its licence before distributing your presentation.
